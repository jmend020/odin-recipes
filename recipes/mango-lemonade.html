Mango Lemonade

On a hot and sunny summer day, what better and refreshing drink to grab than a tropical mango lemonade! Four simple ingredients make this recipe a summer staple among many families, including mine!

Ingredients

  • Mangos: 2 cups of chopped ripe mango or fresh mango puree.
  • Lemon juice: 1/2 cup of freshly squeezed lemon juice.
  • Sweetener: 1/2 to 1 cup of simple syrup or sugar adjusted to your taste.
  • Water: 2 to 4 cups of cold or filtered water.

Steps

  1. Peel and dice your mangoes, then blend them until smooth.
  2. Pour the mango puree into a large pitcher.
  3. Stir the fresh lemon juice and cold water.
  4. Add Simple syrup slowly, tasting as you ggo until it reaches your preferred sweetness.
  5. Put the pitcher in the fridge for an hour, then serve over ice.
