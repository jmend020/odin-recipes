Cheesy Margherita Pizza

A nice and fluffy crust, tasty tomatoes and melted mozzarella cheese, garnished with basil. A very simple but yet delicious take on a classic Margherita Pizza that will surely impress even your pickiest of guests! This recipe makes one 12 inch or 16 inch pizza with a cook time of about 10 minutes! Feel free to make any adjustments to this recipe!
