Cheesy Margherita Pizza

A nice and fluffy crust, tasty tomatoes and melted mozzarella cheese, garnished with basil. A simple recipe for a not so simple taste! This easy recipe uses quality ingredients like San Marzano tomatoes, soft mozzarella and fresh basil to really bring out the beauty of this classic pizza.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • 3 ounces of crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp of extra virgin olive oil
  • 9 ounces of Classic Pizza Dough
  • 3 and a half ounces of fresh mozzarella, torn into pieces
  • 1 Bunch of basil

Steps

  1. Fire up your oven.
  2. Use a small amount of flow to dust your work surface and pizza peel.
  3. Stretch the pizza dough ball out to 12 inches and lay it out on your pizza peel.
  4. Top with sauce, followed by the mozzarella and olive oil, sspreading both evenly on the dough and leaving room for the crust.
  5. Slide the pizza off the peel and into your oven and cook for 5-10 minutes. Make sure to rotate the pizza every minute!
  6. Once fully cooked, remove the pizza from the oven and top with fresh basil leaves.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
